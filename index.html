How we work

From the field to production

  1. 01

    Understand the ground

    Who uses it, on what phone, on what network, paying how. We agree goals, constraints, milestones and a timeline.

  2. 02

    Prototype the risky part

    Architecture and a working slice of the hardest piece — a payment flow, a model, a device — before committing to the full build.

  3. 03

    Build and test

    Iterative delivery with regular demos, automated tests, continuous deployment and documentation.

  4. 04

    Operate and improve

    Hosting, support and iteration after launch, guided by how the product is actually used.
