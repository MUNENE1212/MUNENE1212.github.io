How we work

From the field to production

  1. 01

    Understand the ground

    Who uses it, on what phone, on what network, paying how. Decisions start there, not with the stack.

  2. 02

    Prototype the risky part

    A working slice of the hardest piece — a payment flow, a model, a device — before committing to the full build.

  3. 03

    Build for production

    Tests, continuous deployment, monitoring and documentation, so the system can be run and handed over.

  4. 04

    Operate and improve

    Hosting, support and iteration after launch, guided by how the product is actually used.
