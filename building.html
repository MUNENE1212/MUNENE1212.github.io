How it works

Three steps, money protected throughout

  1. 01

    Find a verified fundi

    Customers search by trade and area. A fundi shows as verified only after an admin has checked their ID.

  2. 02

    Pay into escrow

    The customer pays the agreed price by M-Pesa. It is held in an escrow wallet, not by the fundi and not mixed with Emen's money.

  3. 03

    Released when done

    When the customer confirms — or three days after completion with no problem reported — the fundi is paid. Disputes freeze the money until resolved.

The fundi keeps about 91% of the price; the platform fee is 7.5% plus VAT, taken from the fundi's share. How the escrow works →
